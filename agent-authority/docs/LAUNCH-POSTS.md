# Launch Posts

## LinkedIn

We just open-sourced Agent Authority.

**Give every AI agent a permission slip.**

Passport = who the agent is.
Contract = what it may do.
Gate = whether the action is allowed.
Box = where it runs.
Ledger = what happened.

The project is a developer preview for portable identity, task-scoped authority, approvals, delegation, policy enforcement, and evidence.

It is intentionally framework-neutral and does not require a model provider.

We are looking for people building agents who will try to break it, integrate it, and tell us what is missing.

https://github.com/prayingperceptions/agent-authority
